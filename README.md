## ✨ Features

- 🔄 **Write Once, Reset Daily**: Define your master routine once and get a clean checklist reset every morning.
- ☀️🌙 **AM & PM Schedule Structure**: Tasks are organized into **☀️ AM Schedule** (12:00 AM - 11:59 AM) and **🌙 PM Schedule** (12:00 PM - 11:59 PM).
- 🗑️ **Delete Option on All Tasks**:
  - Delete one-off tasks directly.
  - Delete routine tasks either **"for this date only"** or **"permanently from master schedule"**.
- 📅 **Date-Accurate History**: When you go back to past dates, tasks created later will **not** appear on earlier dates.
- ⏰ **12-Hour AM/PM Time Format**: Display task times in 12-hour format (e.g., `6:30 AM`, `2:00 PM`) or 24-hour format.
- 📌 **One-Off "This Date Only" Tasks**: Add ad-hoc tasks for a specific date without affecting your routine.
- 🔥 **Habit Streaks & 7-Day Matrix**: Track your consecutive streak, best streak, and weekly consistency.
- 🎉 **100% Celebration**: Confetti animation and harmonic fanfare when you complete all daily tasks.
- 💾 **Local Persistence & Privacy**: Offline and stored in your browser (`localStorage`), with JSON Export/Import support.
